Tennis Fantasy 🏆🎾
Compete against friends in a season-long fantasy tennis league – just like NFL fantasy football!
Draft real ATP/WTA players, build your roster, and earn points based on their tournament performances. Head-to-head matchups, playoffs, and bragging rights await!

Features

Player Drafting: Live or offline drafts to select your team of professional tennis players.
Real-Time Scoring: Points awarded for wins, sets, aces, breaks, and more – updated from live tournament results.
Head-to-Head Leagues: Weekly matchups against other managers, with customizable scoring rules.
Roster Management: Waivers, trades, and lineup setting for major tournaments (Grand Slams, Masters, etc.).
Leaderboards & Playoffs: Track standings throughout the season and compete in postseason playoffs.
Mobile-Friendly: Manage your team on the go.

Inspired by NFL fantasy leagues, but tailored for the excitement of professional tennis!
